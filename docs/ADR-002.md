# ADR-002: Evaluation and Consolidation of Document Digitization Approaches

**Status:** Superseded / Consolidated into Architecture Reference  
**Date:** 2026-10-24  
**Author:** Engineering Team  
**Relates to:** [ADR-001](ADR-001.md), [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md)  

---

## 1. Context

Following the initial release of the Image to PDF converter (ADR-001), users tested physical document photos (contracts, book pages, forms). The primary feedback was that raw camera photos—even when made searchable with dual-layer text overlays—retain dirty shadows, uneven room lighting, and yellowed paper.

We set out to investigate whether we could achieve a flatbed-scanner quality result: a clean, pure white digital document while preserving the authentic structure of the physical page.

---

## 2. Decision

We created dedicated experimental branches to implement and evaluate four technical approaches:

1. **Approach 1 (`feature/sandwich-pdf`):** Dual-Layer Sandwich PDF (Adobe Scan style). Original photo background + invisible text overlay at exact word coordinates.
2. **Approach 2 (`feature/markdown-digital-typesetter`):** Semantic Block Typesetter. Gemini Flash extracts structured blocks (`HeadingBlock`, `ParagraphBlock`, `TableBlock`, `ListBlock`, `QuoteBlock`) and reflows them onto a standardized A4 page with TrueType Roboto.
3. **Approach 3 (`feature/whitened-sandwich-nlp`):** Background Whitening Filter. Pillow Gaussian illumination division + invert + autocontrast to bleach the photo background while keeping dark text ink.
4. **Approach 4 (`feature/whitened-sandwich-nlp` - Milestone 4):** Spatial Pure White Layout with LLM Alignment. Pristine `#FFFFFF` vector canvas + Gemini Flash multimodal vision to detect centered titles, right-aligned signatures/dates, first-line paragraph indents, and leveled baselines to neutralize camera tilt.

---

## 3. Results & Evaluation

- **Approach 1 (Sandwich):** Failed user requirement because camera shadows and yellow paper remained visible.
- **Approach 2 (Flow Typesetter):** Failed user requirement because reflowing text discarded the authentic spatial layout and physical line breaks of the source document.
- **Approach 3 (Whitening Filter):** Failed user requirement because raster illumination subtraction created halo noise around text and degraded faint accents without achieving a true vector digital white canvas.
- **Approach 4 (Spatial White Layout):** Closer to the target aesthetic, but physical camera lens distortion (trapezoidal perspective, book spine curvature) and font metric differences between digital Roboto and source print fonts caused subtle baseline drift and margin overflows.

---

## 4. Final Disposition

- All experimental feature branches (`feature/sandwich-pdf`, `feature/markdown-digital-typesetter`, `feature/whitened-sandwich-nlp`) are decommissioned and cleaned up.
- The repository is consolidated on the `main` branch.
- The detailed architectural analysis, trade-offs, and lessons learned are formalized in [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md).
- Future work will prioritize **computer vision geometric dewarping (homography/mesh rectification)** as a prerequisite before OCR or layout synthesis.
